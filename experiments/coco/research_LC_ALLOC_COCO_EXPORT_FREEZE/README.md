# Historical COCO export contracts

The schema, export configuration, candidate identity contract, runner contract
and QA specification are unmodified historical files. Their original hashes,
local dependency references and namespace definitions are retained. They are
records of the executed COCO adaptation/export protocol, not newly issued
execution authorization, public download links or a modified M11 method.

The owned runner/validator source is published separately in
`../research_LC_ALLOC_COCO_CANONICAL_EXPORT/`. Raw images, GT, detector weights,
canonical candidate/native assets and private payloads are not included.
